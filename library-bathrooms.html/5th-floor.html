Full Review:

I was feeling a bit self conscious about walking in and out of bathrooms and basically messing with everything in it, so I honestly felt a bit better seeing the little plastic butterfly on the sign. Made me feel like I was meant to be floating from bathroom to bathroom. The mounted sign outside the bathroom was flipped from where it normally is, which completely threw me off at first. The Braille on the bathroom placard at the entrance appeared to be correct, but for some reason, there was no room or floor number for the bathroom even though that information was available on other floors.

It led into a row of stalls, with the accessible stall being closest to the entrance. As always, there was no emergency call system (on why that can benefit disabled bathroom users, check out the link at the bottom). The stall had all the typical bathroom amenities stocked up, and it had a box for period products. There was a hook to hold bags and a bag platform, which is cool. However, what should have been a typical bathroom experience was shattered when I flushed the toilet and was met with a cacophony of water splashing and flecking up onto the seat. It was much more powerful than any other bathroom on campus for some reason. When these toilets, by default, already tend to be so loud they hurt some people's ears, finding one somehow being even louder was… interesting.

The goddamn crater sink is back. I don't like the crater sink. Moving on..

There were sink soap dispensers that don't work, but mounted ones that do. None of the air dryers worked, but the paper towel dispenser did. There's a lot of give and take when it comes to these bathrooms. This brand of paper towel dispenser has instructions in English and Spanish, which was cool, but it drew attention to the fact that all of the other signage in the bathroom was only in English.

Finally, the tampon and pad dispensers worked just fine. Nice.

Overall, this bathroom is perfectly fine. It has the usual idiosyncrasies of a SJSU MLK, Jr. Library bathroom (looking at you, crater sink and slightly yellow toilet bowl), but I had been lucky enough to use it at a time where things were still nice and in order.
