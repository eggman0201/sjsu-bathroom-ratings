Full Review:

It's fine, I guess. It's similar to the 8th floor bathroom (which you can see the review for here) in terms of layout, including 1 disabled-accessible bathroom. The individual stalls are also laid out identically to the 8th floor bathrooms, with a bag-holding platform and disposal bin for period products. (2/4/2026 11:18AM update: the platform works, but it leans down slightly, so anything that requires balancing, like a cup or a water bottle, will slide).

Like the 8th floor bathroom, the toilet paper and seat covers were nicely stocked, and the toilets were relatively clean, but there was something a bit bizarre about the water: it was tinged yellow. I don't think this was urine, since the water was still yellow after flushing, and the next stall over was also slightly yellow, and there was a bit of calcium buildup on the toilet bowl. I have no idea why this toilet water was slightly yellow. Also, there was a bit of shmutz on the seat, which made the well-stocked seat covers a welcome feature.

These sinks are weird. They were fully metal, which gives me a vague sense of dread and unease, and the third sink from the left was… weird. Instead of the usual bowl-like shape, it looked like a crater. I can only imagine this was created as a freak accident, sick prank, or ill-thought out art project because it did not add any aesthetic value nor did it make hand-washing a more pleasant experience. As a matter of fact, I began to wonder if it was meant for a toddler considering how small the space for water was, but it was entirely too high up for any small-handed human to reach. Very offputting. Very bad.

Not a single air-dryer out of the available 3 worked. Not that I mind; I consider air-dryers to be nonsense, but I find it strange to have features that don't work, much less 3 of them. There was a paper towel dispenser that worked just fine, so there was no harm, no foul, here.

And now comes the question of whether the pad and tampon dispensers work: yes. Big fan of that. At this point, though, I realized my greed in pocketing these, so I think I'll start leaving them back so that people know these dispensers, in fact, work.
