About Us

Why Rate Bathrooms?

SJSU Bathroom Ratings started as a non-comprehensive guide for bathrooms worth using in San Jose State University by the students, for the students. Not all bathrooms are created equal, and people deserve to know what to anticipate as they walk into a bathroom so they know how to act accordingly. Need to paint a toilet bowl bad? This site will tell you which toilets have the flush power to wash away your pain. Need an emergency pad or tampon? We'll let you know which bathrooms have them in stock. Worried about putting your bags on the ground? We'll tell you which bathrooms have stalls that care about the students. Want to share your own thoughts on a particular bathroom or stall? Let us know, and we'll put your reviews on the site. This isn't a democracy, but we might consider your opinions if we can chop it up and throw it into the void.
